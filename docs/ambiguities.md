# Ambiguities

Places where the datasheet (Rev. 7) is silent, inconsistent or typed wrongly, and what the driver does about each.
The driver is safe under every reading: each row is a documented behaviour, and where the chip could behave in more
than one way the host-test model has a knob (a field of its `Readings` structure) that runs the other readings, or the
row says that there is none and why. The
numbers C1 to C59 are the ones the [tests](testing.md) cite. A reading marked "Resolved" was decided against the
safe side; the rest are open until someone measures the chip.

Table, section and page numbers are the datasheet's own (`Table n`, `S` for a section, `p` for the printed page).

## How to change a reading

- The knob of a row is a field of `pf1550_test::Readings` in [`tests/mock_pf1550.hpp`](../tests/mock_pf1550.hpp). A
  test that sets it runs the driver against the other reading.
- When a bench measurement settles a row, change the model's default, keep the knob for the other reading, and say so
  in the [changelog](changelog.md).
- Nothing here is verified on hardware.
